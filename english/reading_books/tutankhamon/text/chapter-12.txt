CHAPTER XII. HORROMHEB'S RAIN
I was heartbroken. He missed his granddaughter very much, and he became very weak.
The old man is dying, thought Horomheb. Now it's my turn to be pharaoh.
After I's death, Horomheb put his mummy in another small tomb in the Valley of the Kings.
The great pharaoh's tomb, originally intended for Tutankhamun, was now ready, but Horomheb didn't
want to bury I in it. He wanted to keep that tomb for himself. So the royal artisans prepared the
tomb for the new pharaoh, King Horomheb, and they painted the walls with pictures of the great
commander in battle. Finally, Horomheb's plan succeeded, and he was pharaoh of Egypt.
But King Horomheb still wasn't happy. His people remembered the young Tutankhamun,
and they were still sad about his terrible accident. Horomheb was furious about this,
and he cut the young pharaoh's name from the great book of pharaohs.
Now everybody will forget about Tutankhamun forever, he said.
Horomheb got his wish. People forgot about Tutankhamun for thousands of years. But one day,
in 1922, an English archaeologist found a small insignificant tomb in the Valley of the Kings.
There, he discovered a fan of ostrich feathers and the treasures of a young pharaoh.
He opened the sarcophagus and found the body of Tutankhamun.
Finally, the world learned the truth about the boy pharaoh Tutankhamun and the terrible plan to kill him.
