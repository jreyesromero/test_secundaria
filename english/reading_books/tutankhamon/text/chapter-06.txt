Chapter 6 The Secret Meeting
Rami's father organised a secret meeting between Horamheb and his son.
Horamheb explained his plan to kill Tutankhamun.
Will you help me?
He asked Rami.
I'll pay you very well.
What do you want?
Gold?
Silver?
I hear you're a brave fighter.
I'll give you a high position in the army.
Can you kill Tutankhamun?
Rami felt very important.
The future of Egypt is in my hands.
He thought.
Horamheb's plan is very dangerous,
but Egypt will be stronger without Tutankhamun.
And I'll have a new exciting life.
What's your answer?
I must think about it for a day.
I'll give you my answer tomorrow.
Rami thought about Tutankhamun.
He was more envious of the Pharaoh than before.
He was in love with Anka Sanamun,
but Tutankhamun was married to her.
It's not fair, thought Rami.
The Pharaoh has got everything.
A beautiful wife, a luxurious palace and money.
And what about me?
I live in a small room next to the stables.
I brush the horses and prepare the chariots for the royal parades.
I hate my life.
Rami went to Horamheb and gave him his answer.
I'll help you, sir, he said,
but I want a lot of money.
Horamheb was happy and he accepted this.
The next morning, Rami cleaned the stable floor
and imagined his new life.
I'll have a lot of money and I'll wear expensive clothes.
He thought.
I'll have a big house with beautiful things
and everybody will respect me.
And after Tutankhamun's death, I'll marry Anka Sanamun.
