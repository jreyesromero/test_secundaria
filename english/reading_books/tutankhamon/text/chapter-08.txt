Chapter 8 The Accident in the Desert
Anka Sanamun's life was very different as a queen. She was alone in the palace all day.
She never saw her friends and she was bored. One day she decided to ride into the desert.
She went to the stables to get her horse. Rami was at the market so another servant helped her.
Anka Sanamun took her horse outside but he walked badly. She examined his leg
and saw a small open wound.
Poor thing, I can't ride you today. She said.
She returned the horse to the stable. There Anka Sanamun saw Tut's hunting chariot
and she had an idea.
It will be fun to take the chariot. She thought.
The servant prepared two horses and she rode the chariot slowly into the desert.
Tutankhamun finished his meetings and went to find his wife.
Anka Sanamun is riding in the desert, Queen Kya said.
Tutankhamun took a strong brown horse and rode after his wife. Soon he saw the chariot.
Anka Sanamun, wait for me. He shouted. His wife saw him and waved.
Suddenly a snake frightened Anka Sanamun's horses and they started to run.
The chariot raced across the desert and the Queen was terrified.
Help! Help! She shouted.
Stop the horses!
Tutankhamun rode quickly after her. He rode next to the chariot and jumped onto one of the horses.
But then the front wheel fell from the chariot.
Help me!
Called the Queen as the chariot fell to the ground.
Tutankhamun's leg was under his horse and the pain was terrible.
Then he became unconscious. Soon everybody heard the terrible news.
The pharaoh was badly injured and unconscious from his fall.
After three days Anka Sanamun began to recover but the doctors were very worried about her husband.
They put herbs and medicines on his leg to stop the infection but it didn't help.
The infection was dangerous and he had a very high temperature.
Queen Kya sent for the best doctors in the country and they tried every new technique.
But nothing helped.
We must prepare for the worst.
I told the government officials.
The pharaoh is dying.
Anka Sanamun was very weak and Queen Kya didn't want to worry her.
Tut is recovering.
She told her.
But two weeks later she came to tell her son's wife the bad news.
Tutankhamun was dead.
