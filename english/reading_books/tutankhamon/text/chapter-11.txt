Chapter 11 – Horamheb the Hero
For the next two years, Horamheb's army was at war with the Nubians.
Rami was now an important officer in the army, but Horamheb didn't trust him.
Rami is very stupid, he thought.
One day he'll talk about Tutankhamen's murder, and everybody will know the truth.
So Horamheb sent Rami on a very dangerous mission.
The Nubians killed him and threw his body in the river.
Now our secret is safe, thought Horamheb.
After the war, the victorious army returned to Egypt.
They brought many Nubian treasures and slaves with them.
Horamheb became a great Egyptian hero, and the people loved him.
How can I thank you for your great service to Egypt?
I asked him.
I want only one thing.
Replied Horamheb.
Your granddaughter, Ankasanamun.
I was very surprised.
Do you love Ankasanamun?
He asked.
Can you make her happy?
Yes, I can.
Your granddaughter is very unhappy now.
Horamheb said.
She never leaves her room.
She can't cry for Tutankhamen forever.
It's time for her to marry again.
I agree with Horamheb.
Thought I.
Ankasanamun must marry and start a family.
I don't like Horamheb, but he's an intelligent and important man.
Maybe he will make my granddaughter happy.
I went to discuss this with Ankasanamun, but she was horrified.
No, no, I won't marry Horamheb.
Please, don't make me marry this man-grandfather.
She cried, but I didn't listen.
You must obey me, he said.
You will be Horamheb's wife, and you will give him a son, the future pharaoh of Egypt.
You must forget Tutankhamun.
He is dead.
The wedding preparations started immediately.
Servants came to Ankasanamun's rooms and made her an elegant wedding dress.
Then they prepared the palace for the wedding.
On the day of the wedding, Queen Kya brought Ankasanamun to the ceremony.
The Queen hated Horamheb more than anybody, and she felt very sorry for her son's wife.
The girl was very thin, and her eyes were red with tears.
Be brave, Ankasanamun, Queen Kya said in a low voice.
You must do this for your grandfather and for Egypt.
The ceremony was very quick, and Ankasanamun didn't look at anybody.
Then the guests moved into the great hall for the celebrations.
Horamheb was very happy. He drank wine and watched the dancing girls.
He didn't talk to Ankasanamun, and she left the room very quietly.
Nobody saw her go.
At 12 o'clock, Horamheb went to look for his wife, but she wasn't anywhere in the palace.
Find my wife! he shouted to the servants.
They looked everywhere, but they didn't find her.
Nobody saw or heard from Ankasanamun again.
