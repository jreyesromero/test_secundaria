Chapter 3 Life in the Palace
The young Pharaoh and his mother moved to a beautiful new palace in Memphis.
I lived near the palace and spent a lot of time there.
He gave Tut lessons every day.
The young Pharaoh learned about his country and his people.
He also learned about his enemies, the Nubians.
Tut started to go to government meetings.
At first he didn't have much responsibility.
He sat next to I and I made all the decisions.
Tut hated the meetings.
He preferred to play with I's granddaughter, Anka Sanamune.
The children spent a lot of time together.
They played Senate, Tut's favourite board game,
and they collected fruit from the trees.
It was much more fun than being a Pharaoh of Egypt.
Sometimes, Tut played with the stable boy, Rami.
They liked wrestling and sword fighting.
Rami was a good fighter because he often practised with his father,
a soldier in the Egyptian army.
Rami usually won the fights with Tut and he felt very important.
He was envious of the young Pharaoh
because of his luxurious life and his beautiful toys.
Horamhab often visited the palace to talk to I.
Sometimes he saw Tut playing with Anka Sanamune or with Rami.
He noticed that Tut always lost the fights with the stable boy
and that he preferred to play with the girl.
Horamhab wasn't surprised.
He thought Tut was stupid and weak.
